Anthropocentrism

A torn strip of white LEDs
Swirling in salty currents
And stirring detritus
Sits on ocean floor

Dragged down by lead-acid battery
Itself rent by boaters' crash
Six drunkard sailors
Daddy's yacht

How long
Can they stay lit
Salt corroding seeps in
Shattered plastic walls surround

What? LEDs don't care
Nor do the fish full of lead
Neither lonely nor cold nor sad
Are the lights, but flickering, now gone
