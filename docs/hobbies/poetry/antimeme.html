Antimeme

Within the air suspending droplet o'er lake
Of liquid methane it Saturn's moon Titan
Sextillions of superposed chiralities
Subtend unobserved curtains of cellulase

Here thunderous fiberglasses stricken and
Cargoers' reactant riverbeds befield a
Trunk splattering knarls against said barrier axiom
Until at Mach 12 the fibrils disintertwine

Somereal further singular parabaloids and
Aperiodically cobbled spinor lattices anneal
Whose ternary logic would implode relativities
Metacausing nondisclosure lemmas thereof

Domains unamenable to abstraction
Extralogic has no truth value

I originally started this one because I liked the feeling of the words I was using. There is some generalization of truth about metaphysics (as the study of what realities other than our own might exist, with different physical laws). Still mostly navel-gazing.
