Spruce Knob

A towering sea of brooms,
Red static lint
Amidst translucent greys,
Foreground, black invert lightning,
All yearning skyward.

Interleavedly rippling spindles
Clatter;
Countermelody: ice,
Harmony: pink cirrus,
Air,
Stone.

Rough loamy tumults
Green-golds percolate
Burst,
Atoms soaked from depths by
Hyperbolic sunshade.

Grass tendrils,
Soft quilted stubble;
Wind-kiss,
Timeless rocks;
Borderless.

Clarity;
No-mind.

After a week of heavy zazen meditation, I visited the dark sky park Spruce Knob in the West Virginia mountains. It was like getting my aesthetic nose broken; the violently beautiful summit caused me ego death.
