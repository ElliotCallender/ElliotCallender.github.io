Intolerable

TW: S/A, possible gender dysphoria.

A feeble, tawny-scalped dementee
Rips fibrous slimy intestines from a gourd,
His left eye-socket feotid,
Cataracts the right.

The pumpkin screams,
An alto rasp crammed into shrill über-soprano lingerie,
Something about rape, dysphoria, gore;
Luckily the Hero has earplugs.

A sensible American patriot
Confined to headaches in hazy fluorescence;
Buzzwords disguise
Despair, loneliness, fear of death.

Gregor awakes lacquered and skittering,
Forced to eat papery dung,
But worse the reverberative horror:
Family poison and stomp.

Could-have-been-nuggetized,
A chickadee stumbles of sprained ankle.
Keen fellows peck out his eyes
And spleen.

Wasting-diseased doe starving,
Her skin-flag fluttering effuses globs,
She will take four thousand three hundred twenty hours to die
Among trillions others, unseen, why?

Rage, rage against the dying of the light.

Fight for the augere spring,
Eudaimonia for our children,
Fresh spruce-flavored mountain air,
Longer lives filled with more hugs.

Cherish and protect the frail spirit,
Cabin across blizzarding tundra,
Flame kindled amidst monsoon,
Friendly hellos in bleakest winter.

Needn't lie that the world is sane or safe,
Rather break the horror as best you can.

Took a road trip across the East Coast in F2024. I spent a few weeks camping solo, and wrote this in New Hampshire just before the presidential election.
